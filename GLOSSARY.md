# Mustafa the Athlete

The brand website for Mustafa, a personal trainer in central London. Its job is to be found by people searching for a personal trainer nearby and to turn them into Enquiries.

## People

**Trainer**:
Mustafa, the one personal trainer behind the Mustafa the Athlete brand (handle style: MUSTAFATHEATHLETE).
_Avoid_: Coach, PT, instructor

**Client**:
A person Mustafa trains, now or in the past.
_Avoid_: Customer, member, user

**Prospect**:
A visitor to the website who is not yet a Client.
_Avoid_: Lead, visitor, user

## Training

**Service**:
One kind of training Mustafa offers: Strength & Conditioning, Sport-Specific Training, Calisthenics, Body Recomposition, Weight Loss, Mobility & Flexibility, or Rehabilitation.
_Avoid_: Package, plan, product, program

**Gym Session**:
Training that takes place at Mustafa's Base Gym.
_Avoid_: Studio session

**Base Gym**:
Fitness First Oxford Street, the gym Mustafa trains Clients from.
_Avoid_: Home gym (confusable with Home Visit), studio, location

**Home Visit**:
Training at the Client's own home, anywhere in London, instead of the Base Gym.
_Avoid_: Mobile training, house call

## Proof

**Testimonial**:
A Client's own words about training with Mustafa, which may include a Transformation.
_Avoid_: Review, feedback

**Transformation**:
A Client's before-and-after photos.
_Avoid_: Before/after, results

**Client Workout**:
Photos or video of a Client training with Mustafa, shown as proof of what sessions look like.
_Avoid_: Showcase, client content

## Contact

**Enquiry**:
A Prospect getting in touch with Mustafa, mainly through WhatsApp, with Instagram or TikTok messages as fallbacks. This is the only thing the website asks a Prospect to do; nothing is sold on the site.
_Avoid_: Booking, lead, sign-up

**Intro Session**:
A Prospect's first session with Mustafa, which the website invites them to book by opening WhatsApp. The Enquiry is the request for it; the session itself is arranged in that chat, never on the site.
_Avoid_: Discovery call, consultation, trial
