About me

I'm Mustafa, a personal trainer based in Marylebone, central London. I've been training calisthenics since 2019, I still play football and I run, so I train myself as an all-round athlete, not just to look good in the mirror.

That's exactly how I coach. Most of my clients are busy professionals who want to feel strong, move well and have energy for everything else going on in their lives. I build every plan around you, your schedule and what you actually want to get out of it. That could be your first pull-up, getting back to sport after an injury or just finally feeling fit again.

Why train with me:

I practise what I coach. Every skill and method I use, I've worked through myself, so I know what it takes and how to get you there safely.

You get a plan that fits your life, not a generic template. I keep things honest, realistic and progressive, so you see real results and they stick.

I actually care about how you're getting on, between sessions as well as in them.

My training philosophy

Live like an athlete. You don't need to be a pro to train like one. It's about building a body that's strong, capable and ready for anything.

  1. Strong

    Strength is the foundation. We build it properly with bodyweight and weights, so everything else gets easier.

  2. Lean

    Good training and realistic nutrition, not crash diets. You'll get leaner in a way you can actually keep up.

  3. Mobile

    Moving well matters as much as lifting heavy. We keep your joints healthy so you can train for years without niggles.

  4. Explosive

    Speed, power and athleticism. Train to perform, not just to look the part.

  5. Consistent

    Small wins, week after week. Discipline beats motivation, and I'll help you build habits that last.
