FAQs

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan and how often you train. Message me and I'll send you my rates.

How far do you travel for home visits, and do you do online sessions?

Home visits cover central London. Send me your postcode if you're unsure and I'll confirm. I offer online coaching as well; get in contact to find out more.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Every plan starts from where you are now. We build the basics first and progress at a pace that suits you.

Do I need a gym membership to train with you at the gym?

Contact me. If the gym isn't for you, we can discuss alternative options.

What happens in the first session?

We talk through your goals, check how you move, and do a first workout pitched to your level, so you leave with a clear idea of the plan.
